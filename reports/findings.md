# Key findings

**Monday afternoons barely cover staff.** Monday 12-3pm averages 5.8 orders an hour, against 9.3 on other weekdays. After 1pm a Monday brings in about $97. Closing at 1pm on Mondays, or running a lunch deal, is worth testing.

**Weekend brunch is the engine.** 9-11am on Saturday and Sunday makes 61% of weekend revenue. Extra kitchen staff in that window protects the busiest hours.

**Cold drinks are seasonal.** Cold drinks sell 3.1x more per month in summer (Nov-Feb) than in winter (Jun-Aug). Order stock and promote them from October.

**Big Breakfast has the thinnest margin.** It keeps only 63% of its price as gross margin, the lowest of the brunch and lunch dishes, on 2,560 sold a year. A $2 price rise or cheaper ingredients would lift profit. Avocado Toast is the biggest single profit earner.
